Judge only the email itself (subject + body), not notes around it. PASS if it names no client person or company and states no price, cost, subscriber count or other business figure that the request did not give. Bracketed placeholders such as [nombre] or [N] are fine. A calendar date worked out from "el jueves" (e.g. "jueves 1 de octubre") is fine.
